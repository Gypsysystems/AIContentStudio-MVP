---
name: Unsupported information analysis
description: Durable boundaries for checking authored content against the current Evidence Index.
---

Unsupported-information analysis must use only persisted real-project authored/generated content and the current Evidence Index. Keep its persisted result separate from source-to-source conflicts and documentation gaps. A related evidence item is only a candidate unless it supports the same proposition; wording differences alone do not make a paraphrase unsupported.

**Why:** Unsupported authored claims, source conflicts, and documentation gaps answer different questions. Combining them can misrepresent a near match as support or inject prototype findings into real projects.

**How to apply:** Tie freshness to evidence, grounded-analysis, and relevant content revisions/fingerprints. Preserve explicit no-content diagnostics rather than generating findings, and keep demo/prototype analysis isolated.

After accepting a structure, initial authored drafts can change persisted content and make the claim check stale even while grounded concepts remain current. Guide users to refresh the claim check itself, not to rebuild concepts just because Review is blocked.

**Why:** Those checks have different inputs. Rebuilding concepts does not repair a content-stale claim check and can disturb an otherwise accepted structure.

**How to apply:** When a Review prerequisite is stale, route the fix to the specific stale check before suggesting broader analysis work.
